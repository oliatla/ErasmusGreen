Issues found in the English source texts

While every worksheet was machine-translated into the five partner languages, the AI translators and our checks compared each sentence with the English original. On the way they found these problems in the English texts themselves — not in the translations.

  • Authors: please fix them in your Word document. The website reads the document again and the change appears in every language.
  • Translators: the translations follow the English as it is written now. When a sentence here is fixed, it shows up again as “not translated” in your language — check it then.
  • Each problem names the text it is about (t… is the text’s id) and suggests a fix. Nothing here has been changed for you.
